# Design — Campus Customs

What changed, and why it should help a shopper stay and buy.

## The shop look

Navy, cream, and coral run through the header, buttons, cards, and footer. The home page uses Libre Baskerville. The rest of the site uses Outfit, with Fraunces on the Campus Customs wordmark. Headings are large, prices are coral, and the pages sit tighter so the clothes show up sooner.

A shopper can tell this is the Broadway store in the first second. The type is easy to read, including the word "from" in the headline, so they do not bounce off a page that looks unfinished.

## Home

A still photo of students walking with a bulldog sits behind the headline and again above the address. The photo does not scroll on its own. Under it, three short reasons to buy lead straight into four featured pieces.

The campus scene gives a reason to look. The pieces are already on the page, with a photo and a price, so the next click is a product rather than a hunt.

## Products

Each card is a photo, a name, and a coral price. The card lifts on hover. The product page shows colors as names and sizes as a compact grid, including a size that is gone.

Shoppers can scan the shelf, search for a hoodie or a college, and see if their size is here before they leave.

## Chat

The corner button says "Ask the shop." The panel uses the same navy, cream, and campus photo as the rest of the site, and product suggestions inside it look like small cards.

Asking about a college, a color, or a size feels like part of shopping, and the answer can put those pieces on the page to buy.
